Infinite Duel

🔊 Tap anywhere to start the tavern music.

Pick 4 heroes for your team. The foe brings 4 too. Your team shares one hand, deck and mana pool; each card is played by its hero. Heroes line up in slots and face each other: attack cards can only hit the front enemy (unless a card pierces or hits all foes). Heal, shield and buff cards ask you to tap an ally.

Hover a hero (or tap the i button) to see its portrait, skills and ultimate.
